# Clarification needed: Oklahoma State University stipend period

The issue reports a **12-month** annual stipend of **17361** (pre-qualification) and **20178** (post-qualification), with a summer amount of **0**.

The linked source (<https://cas.okstate.edu/computer_science/graduate/phd_cs>) actually says:

> "Stipends for half-time assistantships for graduate students are $1,929.00 and $2,242.00, for the academic year (nine months)."

Those are monthly rates, and 1,929 × 9 = 17,361 and 2,242 × 9 = 20,178 — so the submitted figures are **nine-month academic-year** totals, while the `stipend-us.csv` annual-stipend column (and the issue template) require a **12-month** total. AGENTS.md says not to silently accept a 9-month number in the annual field, and not to guess a summer top-up.

1. What is the typical 12-month, pre-tax total that **≥80%** of OSU CS PhD students receive, for pre-qualification and post-qualification? If there is genuinely no summer assistantship pay, please confirm the 12-month totals are 17,361 / 20,178.
2. If summer pay exists, what is the typical summer amount per year that ≥80% of students receive? (We need it for the separate summer column, and to pick the correct `summer-*` label.)

**Minimum evidence needed:** a department funding page, graduate assistantship policy, or offer letter showing the 12-month total (or the academic-year amount plus typical summer pay) and that it applies to a majority of students.

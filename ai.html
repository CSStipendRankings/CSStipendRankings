We use AI (large language models) to help review submissions and draft changes to this website. AI is a tool, not an authority: it can be wrong, and a human maintainer reviews every change before it is merged. This page explains where AI is used and what to watch out for.

Where we use AI

  • The DeepSeek Harness PR Assistant reads new issues and drafts CSV changes as a pull request for maintainers to review. You can trigger a re-run by commenting /dsh (or /deepseek) on an issue or pull request — see Contributing.
  • AI also helps write and maintain parts of this website, including the text on these pages.
  • The assistant is instructed to follow strict rules (12-month, pre-tax figures covering at least 80% of students) and to ask clarifying questions instead of guessing. It does not always succeed.
  • AI is never the final decision-maker: a human maintainer reviews and merges every change that reaches the website.

AI can be wrong

AI-generated content may contain errors. Always verify against a primary source before relying on it.

Typical failure modes include:

  • Incorrect or invented stipend and fee amounts.
  • Figures placed in the wrong column or period (pre- vs. post-qualification, semester vs. summer, 9- vs. 12-month).
  • Stale or dead links, or sources that do not say what the AI claims they say.
  • Wrong labels — for example, marking summer funding as guaranteed when it is not; see Labels & Icons.
  • Confident, fluent prose that is simply false.

Even a correct figure may be outdated, or may describe fewer than 80% of students. Treat every entry as a starting point and check the linked source. See Data & Methodology for how each figure is defined.

What this means for you

Do not use this website as the sole basis for a decision. See the disclaimer on the home page, and read CSRankings' advice for prospective graduate students.

Found an AI mistake?

Please report it. Open an issue or a pull request with the correction and a primary source — public reports are the fastest way to get AI-generated errors fixed. See Contributing for details; the maintainers will not respond to private messages sent to their personal or university accounts.
